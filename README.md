# HF-108-03 Knob Assembly Press Fixture

A custom-designed press fixture developed for knob assembly on the HF-108-03 module. The fixture uses a Destaco 630 press and an adjustable mounting system to provide accurate positioning and repeatable assembly.

## Project Overview

The HF-108-03 module requires a controlled and repeatable process for knob assembly. To support this operation, a dedicated press fixture was designed to accurately position the module and align the press with the assembly point.

The fixture consists of an aluminum base plate, a dedicated module fixture, and an adjustable press base fixture. The press base allows the Destaco 630 press to move horizontally, providing flexibility to accommodate different module sizes while maintaining the required alignment.

---

## Objectives

* Provide accurate positioning of the HF-108-03 module during knob assembly.
* Ensure proper alignment between the module and the press.
* Improve assembly consistency and repeatability.
* Secure the Destaco 630 press in a stable and controlled position.
* Allow horizontal adjustment of the press position.
* Accommodate different module sizes using the same fixture system.

## Design Process

The fixture was designed in **SolidWorks**, focusing on accurate positioning, press alignment, adjustability, and ease of use in a manufacturing environment.

The aluminum base plate serves as the primary mounting platform for the module fixture and the adjustable press base fixture.

The module fixture was designed to locate the HF-108-03 module in the required position for the knob assembly operation. The press base fixture secures the Destaco 630 press while allowing horizontal movement to accommodate different module sizes.

The complete assembly was modeled to ensure proper positioning and alignment between the module and the press throughout the assembly operation.

---

## Manufacturing

The fixture was designed to be manufactured using FDM 3D printing with emphasis on structural stability, accurate positioning, and repeatable operation.

## Assembly

The fixture is assembled by mounting the HF-108-03 module fixture and the adjustable press base fixture onto the aluminum base plate. The module and the press are positioned and secured in the dedicated fixture before the knob assembly operation. Detailed assembly drawings, the Bill of Materials, and manufacturing documentation are included in the Documentation folder.

---

## Results

The final design provides a stable and adjustable solution for the HF-108-03 knob assembly process.

The dedicated module fixture provides consistent positioning, while the adjustable press base allows the Destaco 630 press to accommodate different module sizes. This configuration improves assembly repeatability and provides greater flexibility during the manufacturing process.
